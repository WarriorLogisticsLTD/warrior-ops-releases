Release note

Now live · 27 August 2026

Van checks fill themselves in

The ClearQuote tracker now updates itself every hour. Nobody presses anything for the morning's checks to appear, and a van only ever shows Clear when ClearQuote's finished report says so.

Live now Station managers No training needed

Open the tracker and the day is already filled in — your job is now only the red vans.

What it's for

  • Seeing the morning's van checks without chasing them. The board populates through the morning on its own, so the first look at 9am is already current.
  • Knowing which vans actually need a person. Red means a human has to look; everything else is either fine or still working itself out.
  • Catching new damage against the previous check with before-and-after photos of the same panel, so what changed is visible rather than argued about.

How it works

01

Drivers photograph their vans

Start of shift, from about 6am — exactly as they do now. Nothing changes for drivers.

02

The tracker pulls from ClearQuote by itself

Every hour between 6am and 6pm, one station at a time. Vans go green through the morning with nobody pressing anything.

03

Each van gets an honest status

Clear, or still analysing, or stuck at ClearQuote. A van never claims to be fine just because the report hasn't arrived.

04

The evening sweep reconciles the day

Every van from the whole day is re-read against ClearQuote, so the day ends matching their records — including anything their system finished late.

What changes for you

Station manager

Stop pressing refresh. Work the red vans only — those are the ones ClearQuote is stuck on and a person needs to eyeball.

Drivers

Nothing. Same photos, same time. A driver is never pre-blamed for damage found on their check.

Management

The day's check position is accurate without anyone maintaining it, and every finding is dated to the day the check happened.

Looks wrong, but is correct

A van sat on "Analysing" for an hour

Normal. ClearQuote's AI genuinely takes 30–90 minutes. The old tracker hid that wait by calling vans clear early — the delay was always there, it's just visible now. Past two hours it turns red and asks for eyes.

A damage appeared dated to last Tuesday

The system catching up, not a glitch. When ClearQuote finishes a stuck report days later, the finding is filed under the day the check actually happened. These used to disappear entirely.

A red van — is that the driver's fault?

No. Red means the driver did their check and ClearQuote's system is stuck producing the report. Their photos are on the row, so you can judge the van yourself.

Where the limits are

Known and deliberate

  • Report timing is ClearQuote's, not ours — a stuck van stays stuck until their system finishes it. We chase repeated jams with their team.
  • Nothing is deducted automatically. ClearQuote suggests; a manager confirms; the damage process is unchanged.
  • A flag you set by hand overrides the automatic feed until you hand it back.
