Lend a driver, keep the pay straight

You can now send a driver to help another station for a few shifts without adding them as a new person. One record, their own G-Number, and both stations' pay sheets come out right on their own.

Live now Station managers Replaces the duplicate-driver trick

Find the driver, switch on Cross Station Support, pick the station they're helping — they appear on that station's rota and the pay sorts itself out.

What it's for

  • Lending a driver to a nearby station for a shift or two without adding them as a brand-new person and marking them "left" afterwards — the old way that left a blank G-Number and a mess on the pay sheet.
  • Seeing where your own drivers are when they're away helping — their rota row shows the days they're at the other station, so you can plan their week around it.
  • Getting the pay right without touching it. Each station's pay sheet carries only the days worked there, both under the driver's real G-Number, and they're still paid once.

How it works

01

Agree it between managers

"You can have Dan for two shifts, I need him back Thursday" — the same phone call as now.

02

Open the driver and switch on Cross Station Support

In Drivers, find them and hit Edit. Pick the station they're helping and — if you know it — a "back from" date. Leave it blank for open-ended.

03

They show up on that station's rota

At the bottom, highlighted. That station rotas them like anyone else — any van, any route, day of ops, morning assign, the lot.

04

Your rota shows them "Away Supporting"

The days they're away show the other station's code instead of a shift, so you can see where they are and still book their days back home.

05

Bring them home when you're done

Switch it off — or it ends itself on the "back from" date. They drop back to just their home rota.

What changes for you

Borrowing a driver

They land on your rota for the agreed days. Roster them exactly as you would your own — van, route, morning assign, confirms.

Lending a driver

You can see where they are each day and book their return shifts in advance. Their home row stays yours to edit.

Drivers

Nothing to learn. Their confirm message comes from whichever station they're working that day.

Pay & payroll

No extra step. Both stations' sheets carry their real G-Number and only their own days; they're paid once.

Looks wrong, but is correct

The same driver is on two rotas at once

Right. They're on the station they're helping (highlighted at the bottom) and on their home rota under "Away Supporting". It's one record in two places, not two drivers — which is the whole point.

Their name is on two stations' pay sheets

Correct and intended. Each sheet only lists the days they worked at that station, both under their real G-Number. They still receive a single payment.

It won't let me book a day — says "held by" another station

That day belongs to the station they're helping until someone frees it. A driver can only be in one place per day, so book the days that are actually yours and leave theirs alone.

It stopped me booking them a day at the other station

The day was outside the agreed dates. Extend the support period first (open the driver, change the "back from" date), then book it.

Two of us editing across stations and it clashed

If a rota's been open on screen a long time, another station's changes won't be showing yet. Reload the page before editing a driver who's crossing stations, so you're both on the latest.

Where the limits are

Known and deliberate

  • If a driver is away for a whole week, that week's damage repayment collects from their home shifts — so a full week away, it waits for the next week. It's never lost.
  • Their scorecard, coaching and licence / right-to-work reminders stay with their home station.
  • This is our own move between stations — nothing to do with "away team" on the Amazon invoice. That's a separate thing and it's unchanged.
